About the Program

Big Cuzin' is a youth mentorship and leadership development program of United Society Affiliates, built on the belief that every young person deserves access to real guidance from real men who have walked the walk.

The program empowers at-risk youth through structured mentorship, life skills training, leadership development, wellness education, and entrepreneurship — creating a direct pathway from pressure to purpose and potential.

Rooted in community and dedicated to legacy, Big Cuzin' is designed to transform lives by equipping the next generation with the tools, relationships, and confidence they need to lead.

Four Levels of Leadership Development

Level I

Foundations

"Building the Man Before Building the Leader"

Participants Learn:

  • Character Development
  • Respect & Responsibility
  • Goal Setting
  • Emotional Intelligence
  • Decision Making
  • Personal Accountability
  • Positive Peer Relationships

Participants establish a personal foundation for lifelong leadership and success.

Level II

Leadership

"Leading Yourself and Others"

Participants Learn:

  • Public Speaking
  • Conflict Resolution
  • Financial Literacy
  • Community Awareness
  • Workforce Readiness
  • Professional Conduct
  • Civic Participation

Participants become equipped and prepared to take initiative and serve others.

Level III

Community Builder

"Creating Positive Change"

Participants Learn:

  • Community Organizing
  • Volunteerism
  • Mentorship
  • Project Management
  • Workforce Exposure
  • Entrepreneurship Basics
  • Team Leadership

Participants complete community service projects while developing change-making skills.

Level IV

Peer Mentor

"Leading the Next Generation"

Participants Learn:

  • Active Listening
  • Coaching Techniques
  • Role Modeling
  • Professional Leadership
  • Media & Communications
  • Program Ambassadorship
  • Legacy Building

Participants serve as alumni, Peer Mentors, and help guide younger members.

Leadership Pathway: Foundations → Leadership → Community Builder → Alumni Network

Expected Outcomes & Sustainability

Short-Term Outcomes (0–6 Months)

  • Increased Youth Engagement — Greater participation, attendance, and willingness to communicate openly
  • Improved Emotional Awareness — Better emotional regulation, self-awareness, and conflict resolution skills
  • Leadership Development — Increased confidence and participation in team-building activities
  • Financial & Entrepreneurial Awareness — Financial literacy basics, branding ideas, and goal-setting strategies
  • Positive Mentor Relationships — Meaningful connections with adults and stronger peer networks

Long-Term Outcomes (6–24 Months)

  • Reduction in Negative Peer Influence — Become peer leaders and engage in community initiatives
  • Increased Educational & Career Motivation — Pursue further education and entrepreneurship
  • Stronger Community Relationships — Improved family engagement and community collaboration

Volunteer Opportunity

Become a Mentor

One dependable adult can change the direction of an entire life.

Big Cuzin' mentors are community members who show up consistently — providing real guidance, accountability, and encouragement to at-risk youth ages 13–21. You don't need a perfect background. You need a willingness to invest your time and lived experience in the next generation.

✅

Consistency

Committed to showing up — minimum 6-month engagement

🧠

Emotional Maturity

Able to model calm, patience, and professional boundaries

🌍

Community-Minded

Dedicated to uplifting Syracuse's next generation of leaders

👑

Leadership Experience

Any background in coaching, teaching, trades, business, or community service

Mentor Commitment

  • 2–8+ hours per week depending on your availability
  • Weekday, evening, and weekend schedules available
  • Minimum 6-month commitment requested
  • Background check required — a prior record does not automatically disqualify
  • Uphold Big Cuzin' Core Values: Consistency, Leadership, Respect, Accountability, Community, and Growth
