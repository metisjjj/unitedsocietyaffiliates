Publish A Life Program

Your Story. Your Legacy.
Published for Generations.

A powerful partnership between United Society Affiliates and Upstate Printing to help individuals turn their stories into professionally published books.

Program Overview

P.A.L. (Publish A Life) is a community-powered publishing initiative designed to preserve human stories, ideas, and lived experiences by transforming them into professionally printed works.

Through education, access, and production support, P.A.L. empowers underserved voices to achieve literary immortality—ensuring their stories live beyond a single lifetime and inspire generations to come.

Mission Statement

To create literary immortality by empowering individuals from underserved communities to publish their stories, ideas, and lived experiences—transforming lives into legacies.

Vision Statement

A future where every voice has the opportunity to be preserved, shared, and remembered—where authorship becomes a bridge between generations, culture, and history.

Purpose Statement

The purpose of P.A.L. is to create an "immortality effect" by enabling individuals to document their lives, thoughts, and innovations in permanent, published form—ensuring their impact endures long after their lifetime.

Core Philosophy: The Immortality of Authors

"An author never truly dies."

Words outlive everything—challenging even death itself. For those who have not been afforded the privilege of authorship, P.A.L. offers not just a service, but a right—especially for excluded voices in underrepresented spaces.

A P.A.L. Program Original Series

Chess Wars

Learn Before You Conquer.

An Epic Historical Fantasy Graphic Novel Series

Chess Wars is an original historical fantasy graphic novel series that transforms the world's greatest civilizations into a legendary battlefield where every duel is inspired by the timeless strategy of chess.

At the heart of the saga is Yusuf Avatara, a young warrior born into a powerful legacy after the death of his father, the legendary Chess King Musa Avatara. Guided by the wisdom of his mother, Amora Avatara, and entrusted with the sacred Diamond Stone, Yusuf embarks on a journey across the ancient world to uncover the truth behind his father's death.

Unlike traditional heroes, Yusuf does not seek victory through domination alone. Before every duel, he studies the customs, philosophies, and traditions of the people he encounters. Every civilization teaches him a new way of thinking, and every opponent becomes both a challenger and a teacher.

From the forests of Early America and the mountains of Peru to the temples of India, the castles of Europe, the fortress cities of Mesopotamia, the kingdoms of Africa, and the imperial courts of China, each encounter expands Yusuf's understanding of leadership, strategy, and humanity.

As the battles escalate from Pawns to Bishops, Knights, Rooks, Queens, and Kings, Yusuf discovers that the greatest challenge is not defeating his enemies — it is understanding the forces that shaped them.

The revelation at the end of the first saga changes everything: the man Yusuf has pursued is not the emperor who killed his father, but the emperor's son. Their confrontation ends in a stalemate, setting the stage for an even greater conflict that spans the Chess Wars trilogy.

Why Chess Wars Is Different

  • A unique blend of historical inspiration and original fantasy worldbuilding
  • A hero whose strength comes from wisdom, discipline, and cultural understanding
  • A rich cast of civilizations represented through the symbolism and strategy of chess
  • Neo-Historical Fantasy Realism with cinematic, painterly artwork
  • A trilogy designed for graphic novels, animation, games, collectibles, and educational adaptations

"Learn Before You Conquer."

The Chess Wars universe is built upon five guiding principles:

  1. Observe Before Acting
  2. Learn Before Judging
  3. Adapt Before Attacking
  4. Respect Before Conquering
  5. Strategy Before Strength

These principles define Yusuf's journey and the moral foundation of the entire series.

Whether you're a fan of epic adventures, strategic battles, rich worldbuilding, or unforgettable characters, Chess Wars invites you to experience a story where every move matters.

One board. Sixteen civilizations. Endless possibilities.
