P.A.L. — Publish A Life Program

A United Society Affiliates × Upstate Printing Initiative

Syracuse, New York

Program Overview

P.A.L. (Publish A Life) is a community-powered publishing initiative designed to preserve human stories, ideas, and lived experiences by transforming them into professionally printed works.

Through education, access, and production support, P.A.L. empowers underserved voices to achieve literary immortality—ensuring their stories live beyond a single lifetime and inspire generations to come.

Mission Statement

To create literary immortality by empowering individuals from underserved communities to publish their stories, ideas, and lived experiences—transforming lives into legacies.

Vision Statement

A future where every voice has the opportunity to be preserved, shared, and remembered—where authorship becomes a bridge between generations, culture, and history.

Purpose Statement

The purpose of P.A.L. is to create an "immortality effect" by enabling individuals to document their lives, thoughts, and innovations in permanent, published form—ensuring their impact endures long after their lifetime.

Core Philosophy: The Immortality of Authors

"An author never truly dies."

Words outlive everything—challenging even death itself. For those who have not been afforded the privilege of authorship, P.A.L. offers not just a service, but a right—especially for excluded voices in underrepresented spaces.

Program Components

1

Story Discovery & Development

Participants receive guidance in:

  • Personal memoir writing, poetry & essays
  • Business authorship
  • Idea-to-manuscript development

Delivery: In-person & online content creation workshops

2

Authorship Pathway Facilitation

Participants learn:

  • Copyright ownership
  • ISBNs, formatting & editorial basics
  • Branding & author identity

Focus: Full publishing lifecycle education

3

Print Production & Distribution by Upstate Printing

Services include:

  • Short-run printing & bulk orders
  • Archival-quality physical books & community anthology editions
  • Books placed in participants' hands
4

Legacy & Community Collection

Each published work contributes to:

  • Local libraries
  • Schools & community centers
  • Digital archives for permanent recognition

Partnership with Upstate Printing

United Society Affiliates has partnered with Upstate Printing to provide professional printing services, ensuring that every participant receives high-quality, archival-standard books that preserve their stories for generations.

This collaboration bridges the gap between literary creation and physical publication, making authorship accessible to those who have historically been excluded from traditional publishing pathways.
